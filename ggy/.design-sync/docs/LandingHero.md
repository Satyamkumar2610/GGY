The opening. India is a window cut in a paper sheet; through it, the
Gateway of India. Scrolling dives the camera into Maharashtra until the
window is larger than the screen and the photograph is all that is left.

**Use.** The opening: India cut as a window over the Gateway of India, the title "The Good Governance Yatra" and the route pill. Takes no props: a full-width homepage section that renders its own copy and photographs from the site content. Place it directly inside `<SiteShell>`, in page order, between other sections.

## Example

```jsx
// const { LandingHero } = window.Ggy;  (inside <SiteShell>)
export const Opening = () => <LandingHero />;
```
